This document provides all values mentioned in the manuscript “Heartbeat-evoked responses in MEG/EEG: A systematic review of methods with suggestions for analysis and reporting”. Important Distinction: Studies vs Pipelines

  • Studies: Unique publications (identified by PMID). Each study represents one research paper.
  • Pipelines: Individual analysis approaches within studies. Some studies report multiple analysis pipelines (e.g. different time windows, or statistical approaches).

Power Analysis

Only 5% of pipelines (8/162) had sufficient power to detect the average effect size reported by Coll et al. (2020).

The resulting minimal detectable effect sizes range from 0.18 to 2.37, with a median of 0.88 Hedges’ g.

  • 0.6% of pipelines had enough power to detect Hedges’ g of 0.35 (interoceptive performance meta-estimate)
  • 1.2% of pipelines could reliably detect Hedges’ g of 0.37 (attention to heart meta-estimate)
  • 4.9% of pipelines could reliably detect Hedges’ g of 0.49 (clinical vs. control groups meta-estimate)
  • 24.1% of pipelines could reliably detect Hedges’ g of 0.72 (arousal meta-estimate).

These observations suggest that 75.9% to 99.4% of pipelines may have yielded underpowered and potentially unreliable findings.
