Manuscript Summary

A Systematic Review of Methods Used for Studying Heartbeat Evoked Responses

2025-07-23

Overview

This document provides all values mentioned in the manuscript “Heartbeat-evoked responses in MEG/EEG: A systematic review of methods and suggestions for analysis and reporting”. All sections of this document apart from this overview match the corresponding section of the manuscript. Numbers highlighted in bold are also present in the manuscript.

Important Distinction: Studies vs Pipelines

  • Studies: Unique publications (identified by PMID). Each study represents one research paper.
  • Pipelines: Individual analysis approaches within studies. Some studies report multiple analysis pipelines (e.g. different time windows, or statistical approaches).
